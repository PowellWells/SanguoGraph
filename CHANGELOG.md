# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-14

- Froze the formal graph at 580 people, 358 family relationships, and 189
  source records, with 374 people connected to at least one formal relation.
- Completed searchable source browsing, stable person/relation/source deep
  links, and entity-aware correction and source-suggestion forms.
- Preserved direct-open offline use through the repository-root `index.html`.
- Published project-maintained formal historical data under CC BY 4.0 with
  required attribution to “三国人物关系谱 · SanguoGraph”.
- Kept source excerpts, third-party content, and internal research candidates
  outside the formal-data license and public frontend build.
- Added an automated stable-release metadata and license-boundary gate.

The relationship coverage target remains evidence-constrained: 206 people are
isolated, six above the first-phase target. v1.0 freezes this documented gap
rather than lowering the evidence standard or adding out-of-scope edge types.
